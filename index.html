GLOSSARY

DEMAND is rated 0-10 and shows how eagerly traders want an item.

TREND MEANINGS:

STABLE: constant, steady demand.

RISING: demand is climbing, expect it to keep going up.

HYPE: fairly new item with a demand spike right after release; expect it to level out and become stable within a few days.

DROPPING: demand is falling, expect it to keep going down.

UNSTABLE: uncertain future, could swing either way.
